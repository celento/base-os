Weekend pancakes

A simple recipe for about twelve small pancakes. It is a sample page for the BaseOS Browser.

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar and a pinch of salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 300 ml milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter, plus a little for the pan

Steps

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt in a bowl.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk and melted butter in a jug.
  3. Pour the wet mix into the dry mix and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a buttered pan on medium. Pour in small rounds of batter.
  5. Flip when bubbles appear on top, then cook one more minute.

Ideas

Serve with berries, yogurt or maple syrup. Add a mashed banana to the batter for a sweeter version.
